Old pixels in the transparent overlay are explicitly cleared every paint.

## Health-bar smoothness

Simply increasing drawing frequency was insufficient: actor positions previously arrived with full scene scans. A separate visual reader now samples known living actors up to 120 times per second while needed and PoE is foreground. It validates session/area roots, actor identity, component membership and ownership before publishing. Full snapshot freshness is still enforced; player data and input decisions are not interpolated or replaced.

An off-screen projection initially caused an empty screen position to reach the interpolator. The resulting skipped frames were captured, corrected and covered by a regression test.

Interpolating screen positions also delayed camera motion. Health bars now interpolate world positions with 8 ms default smoothing and use the latest camera for projection. The map/circle delay is unchanged. The user then reported health-bar motion was good.

## Object markers and use state

Rules cover waypoints, area transitions, portals, shrines, strongboxes, ordinary chests (off by default), quests/recipes, Legion and Essence monoliths, Breach, Abyss, Ritual, Expedition, Harvest, Blight, Delirium, Delve, Heist, Ultimatum, Sanctum and other recognizable marked encounters. They require loaded metadata/components and do not expose unloaded objects. Every encounter variant has not been checked live.

Used shrines and opened chests are hidden from verified native flags. Other consumable objects are hidden after an observed available-to-unavailable transition persists for 500 ms, avoiding brief activation lockouts. Initial unavailability alone is not treated as proof of consumption. Availability-based inference is less definitive than a native used flag; dormant objects can remain until their state transition or unload is observed. Waypoints, portals and area transitions remain visible. The existing **Hide used objects when their state is known** checkbox can restore used markers.

The currently used Wetlands shrine reported `used=true`, `available=false`; radar generated exactly one fewer mark with used-object hiding enabled. The independent reference reader reported `IsAvailable=false` from the same shrine byte.
